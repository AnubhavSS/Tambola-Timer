Privacy Policy — Tambola Timer

Last updated: 27 Dec 2025

Tambola Timer respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Tambola Timer mobile application.

Information we collect

Tambola Timer does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app works completely offline and does not require users to create an account or provide personal details such as name, email address, phone number, or location.

How the app works

Tambola Timer is designed solely to:

  • Run a countdown or interval timer for Tambola/Housie games
  • Provide audio and visual cues for game flow

All app functionality happens locally on your device.

Permissions

Tambola Timer may request limited device permissions only to:

  • Play sounds or alerts
  • Keep the screen active during gameplay

These permissions are used strictly for app functionality and not for data collection.

Third-party services

Tambola Timer does not use analytics tools, does not integrate third-party SDKs that track users, and does not share data with external services.

Data security

Since Tambola Timer does not collect or store personal data, there is no risk of personal information being exposed or misused.

Children's privacy

Tambola Timer is safe for all age groups. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
